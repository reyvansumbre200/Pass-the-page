Questions

How do I pay?

After you tap Take it, will reply on WhatsApp with the NGO's payment details. You pay the NGO directly, so the money never passes through anyone else.

What's a "first copy"?

A replica print, not from the original publisher. Same words, cheaper paper. Every listing clearly says whether a book is an original or a first copy.

What's a blind date book?

A book wrapped up with only a few clues on it. You choose it by the hints, not the cover. The title is revealed when you pick it up.

How do I get the book?
